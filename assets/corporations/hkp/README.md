# HALVORSEN-KESSLER PROPULSION (HKP)

Maker of main engines and lateral thrusters. Its visual identity follows an early-1980s aerospace trade advertisement: heavy italic capitals, speed stripes sweeping out of the letters, two flat ink colours.

## Logos

- `logo.svg`: the full name, "HALVORSEN-KESSLER PROPULSION". Use it for manuals, the engine plate and large surfaces. Transparent background; groups `accent` (orange stripes) and `main` (charcoal lettering).
- `logo_acronym.svg`: "HKP", heavy capitals with stripes cut through the lower half. Use it on small parts, panels and thruster housings. Groups `accent` and `main`.
- `logo_source.png`, `logo_acronym_source.png`: the 1024x1024 generated images the vectors were traced from (potrace, one colour at a time, from sub-pixel coverage so the edges are smooth; the acronym's stripes are cut on exact rows).
