# MILTEC INDUSTRIES

Defense contractor: ship weapons and military ship software. Its visual identity follows a 1981 aerospace advertisement style.

## Logo

- `logo.png`: the original logo, 1024x1024 on off-white paper.
- `logo_trimmed.png`: the same logo cropped to its content with a small margin.
- `logo.svg`: vector version, transparent background, traced from `logo_trimmed.png` one colour at a time with potrace. It has two groups, `miltec` (navy) and `industries` (red). Use it for print and large sizes.

The wordmark is "MILTEC" in heavy condensed capitals with horizontal stripes cut through their lower half. "INDUSTRIES" sits below it in red.
