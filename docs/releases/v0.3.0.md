## What's new

**Reprogram your ship in flight.** The ship's programs can be edited and rebuilt in COS while you fly: edit a source with EDIT, assemble it with ASM, take the old program out with its OFF switch and start the new one. Every program on the IRQ 5 chain can now leave it in any order, not only the last one loaded. The fire control is a good place to start: a better filter (one that follows the target's turns as well as its speed) can double the hits close in.

**Your diskette is kept.** What you write on the ship's diskette is saved between sessions. The insurer keeps a backup copy, which you send from the game menu, and a replacement ship boots from that copy.

**Lean in to type.** Clicking the computer's screen moves your view in to frame it while you type. Right-click leans back.

**The ship's hardware manual.** *Ship Systems Technical Reference* (docs/manual) documents every device on the ship's bus, register by register, and how a resident program hooks the chain. It includes a sample program that throws a heat sink when the ship runs hot.

**The station.** The placeholder station is replaced by the art pack's station. It is built from modules, with solid collision: ships bounce off it and take damage, and rounds stop on it. AI pilots steer round it.

**Fixes.** Exported builds showed some models without their paint (the AI fighter, the menu ship, the station, the missiles); they are painted again. Space dust now streams past the main menu's ship as it flies.
