## What's new

**Stations you can go into.** Each station has four big hangars through its ring, with their numbers painted on the doors. Ask for docking on the new COMMS screen (the tablet on the right of the cockpit): the station clears you to a hangar and a landing pad, opens the door and lights the way in. Line up over the pad and it takes the ship; the FLIGHT program shows the pad on the radar screen while you come down. Ask for launch to leave.

**Station radio and laws.** Stations talk to you in recorded voices over the radio. Near a station there is a speed limit (the new parking mode, P, keeps to it) and a protected zone: firing there gets a warning, then the police. Fines and bounties are kept per station; destroy a ship near one and you will be refused and attacked there for a good while.

**On foot.** Docked on a pad, leave the seat and walk the hangar in third person, up the stairs and off the pad. Other players in a session are seen walking there too.

**Money and cargo.** Pilots have credits and a saved record. Buy and sell goods at the market kiosk in the hangar; your ship carries them in its cargo racks, and its computer sees the hold. A storage locker at each station keeps your items. A second station, Kestrel, buys what the first one sells. If you can't pay what you owe, your cargo is seized, then your ship: the insurer lends you an old courtesy ship with green screens to earn it back.

**Screens look like picture tubes:** curved glass, scanlines and a soft glow.

**Graphics settings:** shadows, ambient occlusion, indirect light, reflections and glow, and a sun in the sky.

## Upgrading

- **The ship's diskette is replaced by the new one**, with the new programs (COMMS, the new FLIGHT). Your old diskette is not lost: it is set aside beside the new one as `ship.old.img` (and the insurer's copy as `insurer.old.img`) in the game's user folder, `disks`. From now on this happens by itself whenever a release comes with new programs.
- Games saved with F5 in older versions no longer load.
- Online play needs everyone on this version.
